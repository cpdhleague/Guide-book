<h2 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; text-transform: uppercase; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  Submission Requirements: Deck Techs
</h2>

We welcome submissions for deck tech articles to be featured on **CPDH.guide**. Please adhere to the following guidelines and we'll get your deck tech article live!

* **How does the deck win?** Be detailed about the deck's win condition(s). If you're running a unique combo, explain the steps. 

This is the most important and, potentially, the only thing you need to include about the deck. Outside of the main win condition, you can consider including details such as:
* **Gameplan Execution:** Detail the deck's operational path, describing how the core strategy unfolds across the stages of a game or how it might pivot in different gameplay scenarios.
* **Card Selection:**
    * Outline the broad categories of cards that support the main gameplan 
      Example: "We run as many 6+ CMC creatures as we can so we can utilize the mana generated from Savage Ventmaw each turn." 
    * Provide an in-depth analysis of each category.
      Example: "Because we can cast both of Keleth and Esior from the command zone and all but four spells in our deck with just two mana, I don't believe any ramp is needed, so we don't run mana rocks. Hitting our land drops is very important though, so we're loaded up with 37 of them."
* **Interaction Strategy:** If the deck is designed to interact with opponents, identify the types of opposing decks you are targeting and the answers included in your deck for those interactions.
* **Mana Base & Ramp:** the deck's mana base, which includes your land composition and any dedicated ramp package.
* **Package Variations:** Describe potential alternate card packages the deck could incorporate. Discuss the respective advantages and disadvantages of these variations.
* **Card Considerations:**
    * **Testing:** List cards suitable for the deck that require further testing.
    * **Trap Cards:** Identify and explain cards what readers should avoid playing in the deck, clarifying the reasoning.
* **Playing Against the Deck:** Inform readers how to counter this deck if sitting across the table from it. This involves identifying key weaknesses and critical threats they should target.

## Submission Process

Once your deck tech article is complete, please submit it via email to **[cpdhleague@gmail.com](mailto:cpdhleague@gmail.com)**.

**Please include:**
1.  **Subject Line:** "Deck Tech Submission"
2.  The full article
3.  A link to your full **decklist** on Moxfield or Archideckt
