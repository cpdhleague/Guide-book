The [Cloudy Commons Cup IV: Petal-Palooza](https://topdeck.gg/event/cloudy-commons-cup-iv) was held on Sunday, July 19th, 2026 at the Minneapolis Convention Center, in partnership with North Star cEDH as part of their Summer Classic Tournament Weekend. The Pauper EDH event was run at Competitive REL, with a Masterpiece Lotus Petal awarded to the first-place finisher and cash prizes for additional placements based on attendance.

This page collects the video coverage of the event as it becomes available. Recordings are being released on a rolling basis — roughly one per week — so check back for the remaining rounds and the finals as they go live.

## Winner Interview

The tournament was won by Ken Cockriel, piloting Hawkeye, Young Avenger. You can read our full interview with him here: [Cloudy Commons IV: Ken Cockriel Wins Masterpiece Petal with Hawkeye](https://cpdh.guide/tournament%20reports/hawkeye-cloudy-commons-petal-palooza-interview/).
