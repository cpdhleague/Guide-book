## cPDH Meta Review 2025
This is the first of my annual review articles - at the beginning of 2027 I'll write one that covers 2026. Let me take you back in time to the end of 2025 and see what cPDH looked like then. I have data from 334 competitive games across 11 events with 154 different commanders played. 193 (58%) of all games had one or more of the top 10 commanders in the pod, so most people played with or against them. I will analyze the success of these top 10 commanders based on what we knew on December 31, 2025.

<b>Terminology</b><br>
This meta analysis uses a multiplayer Elo system that I developed to rank all commanders. You can find more details on the Elo system in our [methodology page](https://cpdh.guide/meta/methodology/).<br>
The win rate, games played, events played, event finals, and active players are all based on 2025 data only. <br>
Positive Elo players are the number of active players that increased the commander’s Elo over the year. <br>
Just as commanders are ranked by Elo, players are also ranked by Elo. Players have separate rankings for each of the commanders they play.

This Simic combo partner duo has taken the top rank with a good margin, crowned as the best commander of the year. You don’t get to a near 1,700 Elo without consistency, and Weavers accomplished this while seeing great representation with many players across a majority of cPDH events and reaching the finals in two events. Having more than half of the active players contribute to the commander’s Elo is impressive, especially as they each have a win rate above 50%. This has been a fantastic year for Weavers and they have proved themselves to be the top commander of 2025.

Gretchen is the commander with the highest number of pilots and games played, in part thanks to her being a known budget commander among cEDH players, who joined some cPDH events during the year. Gretchen was the most played commander of the year, however, only a third of those players were able to contribute to the success of the commander. Gretchen’s combo-control playstyle and more complex combo lines require a more nuanced understanding of the PDH specific dynamics. Some new players had four losses and no wins, bringing the overall commander Elo down. She still did well enough to get second place, in part thanks to having the top ranked player. Gretchen is an established and solid commander of the format.

Breeches // Malcolm dropped in popularity and was only played once in all of 2025, compared to 37 games with 6 different players in 2024. Other Izzet combo commanders saw more play, but not more success. Kediss // Malcolm and Malcolm // Rograkh played 16 and 11 games in 2025, and they each lost enough Elo to fall out of the top 10. Malcolm + Red did not have a good year, but considering that all three were in the top 10 at the end of 2024 it remains to be seen whether 2025 was a temporary dip or if the Izzet combo duo will continue to decline.

<b>Conclusion</b><br>
There were three commanders in the top ten with just one player and one event. All three played in the Wanderer’s PDH League, where there was an opportunity to play more games compared to a tournament with 4-5 rounds. All three were also voltron decks, relying on commander damage to finish the game. Did these three voltron commanders simply find a local meta where voltron was the right answer, or do they have potential to win in the larger cPDH meta? Only time will tell. 

Furnace Hellkite, Sailor's Bane, and Tenth District Legionnaire show that you don’t need to pick a commander from the leaderboard to win - there are many hidden gems in PDH waiting to be discovered.

Also worth noting is that three of the top ten commanders have build in discounts. Hollow Marauder provides the best value, but the discount is vulnerable to graveyard hate. Furnace Hellkite builds an artifact board while discounting the commander, but is limited by being mono red. Sailor's Bane is the most reliable as its discount applies both for cards in graveyard and exile.

Half of the top 10 commanders of the year were combo decks, showing that having a sure way to win against three opponents is a huge advantage. The other half was a split between voltron and control decks. We’ll see if 2026 continues to see the same majority of combo decks in the top 10 or if other archetypes gain more ground - I'll write another annual review once 2026 comes to an end. Until then, I hope to see some new commanders from 2026 entering the top 10 and shaking up the meta!
