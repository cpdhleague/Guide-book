The inaugural [Burn Book Brawl](https://topdeck.gg/event/burn-book-brawl) ran on Saturday, August 22nd, hosted by Mean Girls MTG. It was a webcam tournament played online with $1,000 in guaranteed prizing — $400 and a custom commemorative token for the champion, $200 each for second through fourth, and a Burn Book Brawl–stamped promo [[Commander's Sphere]] for everyone who showed up. The event was a blast, which should be expected from the team that previously brought us Sanctuary PDH events. The tokens and stamped promos have been arriving in mailboxes and they're looking great. 

In the audio clip below, I sit down with Tournament Organizer Bobby and Tournament Winner PlumbingSpoon to talk about the event, the recent Zeta and Reality Fracture sets, as well as specific decks we're brewing. Enjoy!
