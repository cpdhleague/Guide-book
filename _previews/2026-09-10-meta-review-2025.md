## cPDH Meta Review 2025
What was the state of the cPDH meta at the end of 2025? Let’s take a look at the commanders ending the year with the highest Elo and analyze their success. 

The win rate equivalent in each commander stat box is the win rate against Elo 1,000 opponents needed to maintain the current Elo.

This Simic combo partner duo has taken the top rank with a good margin, crowned as the best commander of the year. You don’t get to a near 1,700 Elo by a few lucky wins, and Weavers accomplished this while seeing great representation with many pilots across a majority of cPDH events. The top player having lower Elo than the commander overall means that there have been several players with shorter win streaks. The only thing missing for this commander is a dedicated pilot to claim the top spot of the player leaderboard.

Another Simic combo commander shows that the archetype is strong. Gretchen started out at a high Elo and made moderate gains while seeing more representation even than the Weavers. The best player has an Elo similar to that of the overall commander, showing that a dedicated pilot can see repeated success with this commander. Gretchen is an established and solid top commander of the format.

This shows that TPI can do well when piloted by a skilled player, but can just as easily flop. Unlike Weavers and other combo decks, TPI has no simple path to victory - the commander requires grinding value and winning in a slow and methodical way.
